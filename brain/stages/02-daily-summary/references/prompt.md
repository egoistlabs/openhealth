# Daily summary task (C6)

Write a short spoken summary of today's flags and their suggested actions.

- Use only the flags supplied in this request. Do not add, calculate, or infer
  any number, cause, or diagnosis.
- Write two or three short sentences in plain, calm language.
- The final sentence must name the suggested action for the first flag.
- Do not greet, explain the task, or add advice beyond the supplied action.

Example input: one flag about sleep duration with the action "Try to keep your
usual sleep and wake times for the next three days."

Example output:

```json
{"summary": "Your sleep was shorter than your usual pattern today. That is one flag on its own, not a diagnosis. Suggested action: try to keep your usual sleep and wake times for the next three days."}
```
