Breathing rate is the number of breaths a minute while asleep, read by the
crew wearable. The Canadian Space Agency's Bio-Monitor measures breathing rate
and volume on the space station.

For an average healthy adult at rest, a normal breathing rate is 12 to 18
breaths per minute. The change from the person's own normal still matters
most. Application code works out that normal from the previous 14 nights and
sets Stable, Watch or Urgent. The companion must not calculate the status
itself.

On Watch: suggest rest and checking again tomorrow. On Urgent, or with
breathlessness or chest pain: tell the crew medical officer now.
