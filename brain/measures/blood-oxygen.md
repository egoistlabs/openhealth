Blood oxygen, or oxygen saturation (SpO2), is how much of the blood's oxygen
capacity is in use. The crew wearable reads it overnight. The Canadian Space
Agency's Bio-Monitor measures blood oxygen saturation on the space station.

A normal oxygen saturation level is between 95% and 100%. A reading from a
pulse oximeter can be 2% to 4% higher or lower than the true level, so one
reading is a guide, not a diagnosis.

In the app, below 95% shows Watch and below 92% shows Urgent, whatever the
person's own normal. Application code sets the status. The companion must not
calculate it.

At 92% or lower: tell the crew medical officer. At 88% or lower: this needs
immediate medical attention, so contact the crew medical officer now. Do the
same straight away if the person is short of breath, whatever the number.
