Fatigue and headache can have many possible causes. A chat companion cannot
identify the cause, determine severity, or rule out an urgent medical problem.

Follow mission medical procedures and report new, severe, worsening, or
persistent symptoms to the designated medical professional. For emergency
warning signs, use the mission's urgent escalation process immediately.

This reference intentionally offers no diagnosis, medication advice, or
individual treatment instructions.
