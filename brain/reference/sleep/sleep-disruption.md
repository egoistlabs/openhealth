Mission schedules, environmental conditions, workload, and stress can make
sleep difficult. A conversation alone cannot establish the cause of sleep
changes or determine whether a person has a sleep disorder.
The companion cannot tell whether a crew member has a sleep disorder. It can
share this general information, but only the designated medical professional
can assess an individual concern.

A crew member can note what changed, how rested they feel, and whether the
change is affecting daily tasks. Keeping the sleep opportunity and routine
aligned with the approved mission schedule may be useful where that schedule
allows. Do not change prescribed treatment or mission schedules based on this
reference.

For persistent sleep problems, significant daytime fatigue, or concerns about
safe performance, contact the designated medical or mission support team.
