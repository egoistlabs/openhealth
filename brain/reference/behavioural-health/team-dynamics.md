Close teamwork under demanding conditions can make misunderstandings or
disagreements feel more significant. Differences in communication style do not
by themselves establish that a relationship or team is unhealthy.

When appropriate, a crew member might describe the specific situation, explain
its impact, and ask for a practical next step. Choosing a calm time and checking
what the other person meant can help clarify a misunderstanding. Use the
mission's agreed procedures for significant or unresolved conflicts.

This is a reflection aid, not mediation or an assessment of any crew member.
For persistent or safety-relevant concerns, contact the designated mission
support channel.
