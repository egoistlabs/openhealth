The Astronaut Companion AI is a conversational support tool, not a clinician or
therapist and not a replacement for human support. It can help with reflection
and provide source-based general information; it does not diagnose.

This prototype does not establish that conversations are recorded, stored, or
sent to Earth. The application must clearly disclose its actual storage and
sharing behavior before collecting sensitive journal or conversation content.
The companion cannot confirm that a journal is being sent to Earth.
Do not promise privacy, confidentiality, or transmission that the application
has not implemented and explained.
