Isolation and confinement can limit ordinary contact with friends, family, and
other communities. A person may feel lonely even when other crew members are
nearby; loneliness is an experience, not a personal failing.

If it feels useful, consider a small connection that fits the mission schedule:
share a meal, have a brief conversation with a crewmate, or write down something
to share with someone later. It can also help to tell a trusted person what kind
of support would feel welcome.

These are optional reflection ideas, not treatment. If loneliness feels
overwhelming or is affecting daily life, consider contacting the mission's
designated behavioral-health or medical support.
