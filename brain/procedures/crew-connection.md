1. Check the mission schedule and respect each person's work and rest periods.
2. If it suits everyone, invite a crewmate to a short conversation or shared
   activity.
3. Ask what kind of support would be welcome instead of assuming.
4. Respect a person's choice to decline and try another time if appropriate.
5. Use the agreed team process for a concern that persists or affects safety.

These are optional social ideas, not a conflict-resolution protocol or a
replacement for communication with family, friends, or mission support.
