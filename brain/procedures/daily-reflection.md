1. Choose whether you want to reflect today; participation is voluntary.
2. Name one thing that felt manageable and one thing that took effort.
3. If useful, note sleep, mood, workload, or connection in your own words.
4. Choose one small next step, or decide that no action is needed right now.
5. Bring a persistent or safety-relevant concern to the designated mission
   support person.

This is not a clinical screening, diagnosis, or substitute for required
mission reporting. Do not include sensitive information unless the application
clearly explains how it is stored and shared.
