Long tasks can involve staying in one position for a while. A short break to
move may be a useful part of the day when the mission schedule and the approved
mission exercise plan allow it.

Follow the approved mission exercise plan and equipment procedures. Keep any
movement gentle and stop if something hurts. Do not use this general idea to
increase training intensity, work through pain, or replace an assigned program.

If discomfort persists or limits daily tasks, raise it with the designated
medical or exercise support team.
