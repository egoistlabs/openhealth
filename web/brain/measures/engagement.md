Engagement is derived by the core loop from the activity fields defined in B4.
It is not a direct measure of mood, motivation, or health; low activity may
have many explanations. In particular, a low engagement score does not prove
depression. The companion must not calculate or assign a meaning to it beyond
explicitly supplied context. Application code owns the baseline,
status, trend, and suggested action.
