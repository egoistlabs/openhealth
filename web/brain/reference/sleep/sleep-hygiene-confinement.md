In a shared and scheduled environment, sleep opportunities may depend on
mission operations and the needs of other crew members. A predictable,
mission-compatible wind-down routine may help some people prepare for rest.

When practical and consistent with the mission plan, a person might reduce
optional stimulation before sleep, use approved sleep equipment as intended,
and coordinate shared-space expectations with crewmates. These are general
ideas; they do not override operational requirements.

Raise persistent disruption, environmental problems, or safety concerns with
the designated mission support team. This reference does not prescribe a sleep
schedule or treatment.
