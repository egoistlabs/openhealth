The body's daily timing system (often called the internal body clock) helps
coordinate sleep and wakefulness. A
schedule that shifts or differs from a person's usual timing may make it harder
to feel alert at the desired times. This general description cannot determine
an individual's circadian phase.

Follow the mission's approved light, sleep, and work schedule. If sleep timing
is becoming difficult, note the pattern and discuss it with the appropriate
mission support person rather than making a large schedule change alone.

This file does not provide a personalized phase-shifting protocol. Do not use
it to change medication, light exposure requirements, or assigned duty periods.
