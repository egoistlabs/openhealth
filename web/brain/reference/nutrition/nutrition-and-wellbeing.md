Meals can provide structure and an opportunity for social contact, but eating
patterns and needs differ among people and across mission conditions. A shared
meal may be a convenient moment to connect when schedules allow; it is not a
treatment for loneliness or mood concerns.

Follow the approved food plan and any individual medical guidance. If appetite,
food tolerance, or access to meals is a concern, raise it with the designated
medical or nutrition support person rather than making a significant change
based on this general information.
