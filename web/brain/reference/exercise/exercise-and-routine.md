Movement can be one part of a daily routine during confinement, but a
conversation cannot assess a person's readiness, injury risk, or exercise
prescription. The companion cannot make a personal exercise plan.

Follow the approved mission exercise plan and equipment procedures. If a
person wants a small routine change, they should check that it fits the plan
and discuss restrictions or discomfort with the designated medical or exercise
support team.

Do not use this general reference to increase intensity, work through pain, or
replace an assigned program.
