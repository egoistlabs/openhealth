Limited privacy, repeated routines, and shared living space can be frustrating.
People adapt differently, and a difficult day does not mean someone is failing
to adapt.

Optional ideas to reflect on include protecting a small personal routine,
agreeing on shared-space expectations with the crew, and making room for quiet
time when the schedule allows. A practical concern about space, noise, or
privacy is worth raising through the team's usual communication process.

This is general context, not an individual assessment or mission procedure.
Follow the current mission plan and ask the designated support team when a
concern is persistent or affects functioning.
