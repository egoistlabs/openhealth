Living and working in a confined, demanding environment can bring stress,
frustration, worry, or changes in mood. Reactions vary from person to person
and can change over time. This reference cannot determine why someone feels a
certain way or diagnose a condition. The companion cannot diagnose anxiety from
a chat.

For a moment of reflection, a crew member might name what is weighing on them,
notice what is within their control today, and choose one manageable next step.
Talking with a trusted crewmate or designated support person may also help.
These are optional ideas, not a substitute for professional care.

If distress is intense, persistent, or interfering with safe work or daily
functioning, contact the mission's designated behavioral-health or medical
support.
